# Support

Contact https://armotusitio.com.ar/soporte/ or open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/Drako01/orquestivo/issues) with an anonymized reproducible pipeline file, the OS and the extension version. Do not post secrets or private logs. Orquestivo is an unreleased preview without a public release.

Self-service: [docs/USAGE.md](docs/USAGE.md) and [docs/TROUBLESHOOTING.md](docs/TROUBLESHOOTING.md). Security reports: [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
